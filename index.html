How to play

Before you drive: VoiceOver

VoiceOver takes over the screen, so the pedals cannot work while it is on. Set things up here with VoiceOver, double-tap Start race, then triple-click the side button to turn VoiceOver off. Triple-click again after the race to turn it back on. With Automatic speed you only steer, so you can leave VoiceOver on and double-tap Start lights.

Holding the phone, pedals and sounds

Holding the phone

Hold the phone sideways in both hands like a steering wheel, screen facing you. Turn it left and right like a wheel. Wear headphones so you can hear left and right. If your phone turns the screen round while you steer, that is fine, the steering still works.

Pedals

  • Accelerate: hold one thumb anywhere on the screen.
  • Coast: lift your thumbs off.
  • Brake: hold two thumbs on the screen.
  • Pause: tap with three fingers. Tap with three fingers again to carry on.

The sounds

  • Your engine sits where your car is across the track. If it drifts to your left ear, your car is on the left. Pitch rises with speed and drops at each gear change.
  • Kerbs rumble when you reach the edge. Gravel crunches and slows you if you go off. Barriers crash.
  • Bend warnings sweep towards the bend. One sweep is a fast bend, two is medium, three is a tight bend or hairpin, and lower pitch means tighter. A chicane plays one sweep for each part, in order.
  • Brake now is a double beep. A tick in the bend's direction means turn in now. In a bend your car drifts to the outside, so steer into the bend.
  • Rival cars are heard where they are: left or right, ahead or behind. Cars behind you sound more muffled. Their pitch drops as you pass them. The track has three or four lanes, so pull out of a car's lane and go past. Driving close behind a car gives you a slipstream boost on the straights.
  • The start: five beeps for the five red lights, a short wait, then a rising tone: lights out, go.

The announcer only speaks for the start, your position, laps and the result.
