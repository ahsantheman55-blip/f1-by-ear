# F1 by Ear

An accessible Formula One audio racing game for iPhone Safari, made for Ahsan Ali. You steer by turning the phone like a steering wheel and race rival cars by sound.

Play: https://ahsantheman55-blip.github.io/f1-by-ear/

- Steering: DeviceMotion gravity angle around the screen's axis, so it works in both landscape directions and with rotation lock on. Calibrated centre and range, One Euro filter, adjustable sensitivity, dead zone and smoothing.
- Pedals: one finger accelerates, two fingers brake, three-finger tap pauses. Or Automatic speed.
- Audio: Web Audio. Pitch-steadied real F1 engine loops mapped to revs with gear changes, panned by your position across the track. Kerbs, gravel, tyre squeal, barrier and contact sounds. Up to 9 rival engines placed in stereo by distance with Doppler. Bend warnings that sweep towards the bend.
- Two tracks (Monza-style, Monaco-style), 1 to 10 laps, 3 to 9 rivals, three difficulty levels, slipstream, a practice lesson and free practice with spoken notes.
- Plain HTML and JavaScript, no libraries, works offline after the first visit.
